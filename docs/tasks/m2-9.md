## Decisions (MAIN)

### 1. Data and rules (shared)

- `creation.json` gains the lines above:
  - per Remembrance: `image` and `clothing` (LORE's, replacing `outfit`; check every use of `outfit`);
  - `screens` for the three steps' lines, `narrator` for the five Narrator lines, `toneTip`, `refused`;
  - `openingMs` (3000) and `closingMs` (2000): how long the opening and closing Narrator lines hold on black;
  - `turnMs` (600): how long the Appearance figure holds each facing.
  - Validated on load; every line must pass `isDrawable` and the banned-names test.
- `validateKindledName` refuses with LORE's line: a `KINDLED_NAME_REFUSED` constant, read from `creation.json`. `validateIslandName` and the island's other refusals keep `NAME_REFUSED`. `NAME_NOT_DRAWABLE` stays for a name the fonts can't draw.
- "Both filters" (GAME DESIGN) are the safe-letters filter and the offensive-word check, which `validateTypedName` already runs through `isSafe`. The offensive-word list is empty today; filling it is not this task.
- The server's `POST /api/kindled` refuses with the same line (`name-refused`), so the client and the server say one thing.

### 2. The screens (client)

`CreationScene` becomes the three steps, with the logic pure and tested in `game/creationView.ts` (the steps, the focus, what Back and Next do, which Narrator line shows).

**The flow:**
- the opening on black: the Narrator line for `openingMs`, or until any input;
- Remembrance, Appearance, Name, each step with **BACK** and **NEXT** (the Name step's NEXT reads **CONFIRM**). The first step's BACK is greyed. A choice made on an earlier step is kept when you go back and forth.
- After a confirmed name: the closing line on black for `closingMs`, then the start screen as today.
- A refusal shows LORE's line under the field, in the error token, and the step stays.

**The Narrator:**
- One line at the foot of the screen, centred, wrapped, in the body font, muted ink.
- It shows the line the moment calls for: the hover/focus lines on the first two steps, the empty-field line on the Name step while the field is empty, nothing otherwise.
- No speaker label: LORE's Narrator lines carry none in the script.
- ART: "That's the Narrator's voice everywhere, and the UI kit will keep it. If a line runs past one row, wrap it centred to two rows rather than shrinking the font." So wrap centred, at most two rows, never a smaller font. Test that every Narrator line in `creation.json` fits two rows at the UI width.

**Step 1, Remembrance:**
- The screen's line at the top, then four cards in a row.
- Each card has the name, the image line (muted, wrapped) and the clothing line.
- To the right of or under the cards, one figure for the chosen card, in the chosen tone. It draws from `kindled-<remembrance>-sheathed` when that sheet exists, else `kindled-ash-sheathed`, standing `idle` facing south.
- Only the Ash sheets exist today, so every Remembrance shows the Ash figure tone-swapped until its own lands (ART: "Keep it"). ART's jobs bring `kindled-clay-sheathed`, `kindled-tide-sheathed` and `kindled-wing-sheathed` (the five roaming rows in 8 facings) in that order. The figure picks each up with no code change, so make the lookup take any sheathed sheet on disk; it must not be a fixed list.
- Show one figure, for the chosen card, not one per card: four identical figures would say nothing.

**Step 2, Appearance:**
- The four swatches with the tones' names, and the tooltip line under them.
- The figure, larger, turns through the 8 facings: `idle` S, SE, E, NE, N, NW, W, SW, each held `turnMs`, in the chosen tone (`toneTextureKey`) and the chosen Remembrance's sheet with the same fallback.
- Turning by hand (GAME DESIGN): the step has two focus rows, the tones and the figure.
  - On the figure row, Left/Right (D-pad on the pad) turn it one facing.
  - A drag across the figure (mouse or touch) turns it one facing per 24 UI px.
  - Either pauses the automatic turning until the player leaves the step.
  - On the tones row, Left/Right still choose the tone (MAIN: the two can't share one row).

**Step 3, Name:**
- The screen's line, the DOM name field (`formFields.ts`, as today) and the figure standing south.
- The empty-field Narrator line while the field is empty.

**Input:**
- Keyboard: Left/Right step through the choices (or turn the figure on its row), Up/Down, Tab and Shift+Tab move the focus between rows and buttons, Enter is NEXT, Esc is BACK. Typing goes to the name field on the Name step.
- Pad (`MENU_BUTTONS`): the D-pad steps, Cross is NEXT, Circle is BACK. The name still needs the keyboard, as today.
- Mouse and touch: click or tap a card or swatch to choose it, the buttons to move.
- Hover and focus both bring up the hover Narrator lines.

### 3. The Remembrance's sheet, wherever the Kindled is drawn (added 2026-10-19)

ART's job #114 makes `kindled-clay-sheathed`, `kindled-tide-sheathed` and `kindled-wing-sheathed`, one at a time. Each lands as an art PR with its PNG, its JSON and its skin mask (`.skin.png`, like Ash's). Today two things would stop them showing:
- the holding draws every Kindled from `PLAYER_SHEATHED_SHEET` (`kindled-ash-sheathed`);
- optional sheets (`OPTIONAL_SPRITE_SHEETS`) load without their skin mask, so a Remembrance's sheet could not take the clay-tone swap (`toneTextureKey`).

So, in this task:
- Add the three to `OPTIONAL_SPRITE_SHEETS` as Kindled sheathed sheets. They are checked against `KINDLED_SHEATHED_ANIMS` like Ash's once on disk, and their skin mask loads with them when it exists. An optional Kindled sheet without its mask is refused by the sheet test, not drawn untinted.
- One function, `sheathedSheetFor(remembrance)` in `render/characters.ts`, gives `kindled-<remembrance>-sheathed` when it is loaded, else `kindled-ash-sheathed`. Creation's figure and the holding's Kindled (`render/holdingWorld.ts`, from the Kindled's `remembrance` in `/api/me`) both use it.
- Encounters keep `PLAYER_SHEET` (`kindled-ash`): the combat sheets for the other Remembrances wait for M3 (ART).
- A test with a fixture sheet (like the Sparkglass fixture) checks the lookup, the fallback, and the mask path, both present and absent.

This changes "Don't touch the holding" below for this one point: the Kindled's sheet. Nothing else on the holding changes.
