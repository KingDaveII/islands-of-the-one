## Decisions (MAIN)

### 1. Hearing a Name: the transliteration (shared)

Port LORE's reference script to TypeScript in `packages/shared` (`hearName(typed): string`, returning the Old Letters with words separated by spaces, base letters only; `shapeLetters` adds the final forms when drawing). Same input, same letters, every time. The rules, as LORE wrote them for MAIN:

1. Start from the Name as the account rules store it (NFC, trimmed, spaces collapsed). Old Letters typed into a Name are kept as typed. Other scripts are romanized first with their standard romanization once the fonts can draw them; until then only Latin reaches step 2.
2. Fold: lowercase; ß → ss, æ → ae, œ → oe, ø → o, å → o, ð and þ → th, ñ → ny, ç → s, ý and ÿ → y; then strip the remaining accents (é → e, ü → u).
3. Split into words at spaces and hyphens; drop every other non-letter (digits, apostrophes, dots). Inside a word, a doubled consonant counts once (ll → l), and a final h after a vowel is silent (Sarah, Noah).
4. Read each word left to right, longest match first, into consonants (table A) and vowels (table B). y and w are consonants before a vowel and vowels otherwise, and w is a vowel only right after another vowel. A vowel pair ending in y or w breaks before a vowel (in Maya the y is a consonant). c is ס before e, i or y, and ק otherwise.
5. Write each vowel by where it stands in its word: first, inside or last (table B). A word that is a single vowel takes the "first" column.
6. Two identical letters side by side merge into one when either of them came from a vowel.
7. **Close the doors**, across the whole Name, ignoring spaces: where yod touches he, drop the yod if it came from a vowel; otherwise, if the he came from a vowel, cut it as א; otherwise cut the he as ח. Where yod touches yod, drop the one that came from a vowel. Repeat until no door is open.
8. **The lean hand:** if the Name has more letters than the Writ line holds (`lineLetters` in data, 12; ART confirms the number from the tablet), drop the letters that came from inside vowels (י and ו, never a word's first or last letter), last word first and from the end of each word, until it fits. Then close the doors again. A Name that still doesn't fit is the layout's problem (below).
9. A Name left with one letter gets an א in front of it.
10. Final forms at the end of every word: `shapeLetters` already does this.
11. Check the result with the safe-letters filter, **strict**.

The tables are in the reference script (`CONSONANTS`, `VOWEL_UNITS`, `INITIAL`, `MEDIAL`, `FINALV`). Port them as data in the module, not as `if` chains. Where the script and the rules above disagree, the script wins (it produced the book's examples); say so in the PR.

### 2. The island's Name

- **Typed Name:** the same rules as a Kindled name (`validateKindledName`: normalized, drawable characters only for now, 2–24 grapheme clusters, a letter, the non-strict filter on the typed text). Reuse it, generalized to a `validateTypedName` if that reads better.

### 3. The Writ: state and math (shared, pure)

**A new data file, `packages/shared/data/writ.json`, validated on load.** These are GAME DESIGN's starting values:

| Key | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `sliverHours` | 48 | One wild sliver's charge |
| `capacityHours` | 144 | A full Writ (three slivers, "a full Writ holds six" days) |
| `feeds` | `wild-sliver` 1, `sealed-gin` 1, `glass-chip` 3, `deep-glass` 9 | Slivers' worth per item |
| `lowHours` | 24 | Under this the Writ flickers and the chip turns ember |
| `graceHours` | certified 48, uncertified 24 | Dark, but the island still remembers itself |
| `lineFadeHours` | 24 | After the grace, one line fades each such stretch, outermost first |
| `lineLetters` | 12 | Letters a Writ line holds (the lean hand, §1) |
| `linePlots` | 4 | Plots each line tethers |
| `m2Scaffold` | true | M2 builds only; see the grace below |

**The stored state** is the Name, its letters, `certified` (false), `emptyAt` (when the charge runs out; null for a Writ never fed) and the number of lines (1 in M2). Everything else is derived by one pure function, `writView(writ, now, data)`:

- **charge:** `max(0, emptyAt − now)`, in ms and in hours;
- **state:**
  - `new`: never fed. The letters glow ember, and it neither drains nor fades. The island counts as lit, so offline earning continues.
  - `lit`: charge at or above `lowHours`.
  - `flickering`: charge above 0 and under `lowHours`.
  - `dark`: charge 0, within the grace. The Writ reads מת.
  - `fading`: past the grace.
- **darkSince:** `emptyAt`, once it is past.
- **graceEndsAt:** `emptyAt + grace`. The grace is the certified one for a certified Writ. **While `m2Scaffold` is on, an uncertified Writ also gets the certified grace:** GAME DESIGN's M2 table says "After the 48 h grace, line 1 fades on the third day", and nobody can be certified in M2.
- **lines:** each with its plots (`linePlots`) and its state (`lit`, `dark` or `faded`). Line k (1 = the Name's line, the only one in M2) fades at `graceEndsAt + (k − 1) × lineFadeHours`, outermost line first. With one line, line 1 fades the moment the grace ends: the start of the third day for a 48 h grace.
- **words:** the charge in words, for the chip and the panel. Hours / 24 rounded to the nearest whole day gives n:
  - 0: "less than a day";
  - 1: "about a day";
  - otherwise "about n days", with n spelled out.

  The exact time ("41 h 12 min") is for tooltips.

**Feeding, as a pure preview the client and server share, `feedPreview(writ, itemId, count, now)`:**
- Only items in `feeds` can be fed.
- A new or dark Writ starts from now: `emptyAt = now + hours`. A lit one adds to `emptyAt`.
- **Nothing is wasted:** a feed that would carry the charge past `capacityHours` is refused. On an already full Writ the refusal reads "Full.". Fill feeds one item at a time while the next one fits.
- Any feed relights a dark or fading Writ at once. Faded lines come back "one after another within minutes": that is the client's animation (§6). On the server they are lit the moment the feed lands.

Flag in the PR, for GAME DESIGN: with a 144 h capacity, a deep glass (9 slivers) never fits and a humming glass fits only an empty Writ. The rule above follows the book ("A full Writ refuses more, so glass is never wasted by over-feeding"); if GAME DESIGN wants partial feeds, it's a change to this one function.

### 4. The server

- **Storage:** a new table `writs`, keyed by the Kindled (cascade):
  - `name` and `letters` (null until named);
  - `certified` (default false);
  - `empty_at` (null);
  - `lines` (default 1);
  - `created_at`.

  The migration is expand-only. A Kindled's row is created on first use (the first holding request), so existing Kindled get one too. Only the holding module touches it.
- **The settle reads the Writ.** `offlineAllowed(kindled)` becomes the offline horizon from the Writ:
  - new, lit or flickering: the full offline cap, as now;
  - otherwise, the stretch earns unattended only until the moment the Writ went dark: `min(cap, emptyAt − lastSeenAt)`, never less than the online grace (`ONLINE_GRACE_MS`), so hands still work online at full rate and XP (GAME DESIGN: "Dark means idling stops, not that the player is locked out").

  Lock the Writ row in the settle's transaction. Test a stretch that crosses the dark moment: paid up to `emptyAt`, nothing after while away, and paid again for heartbeat-covered online time.
- **Feeding** runs in one transaction:
  1. lock the Kindled and the Writ;
  2. compute the preview;
  3. take the items through `inventory.apply` (reason `feed-writ`, already in `InventoryReason`), from the **store first, then the pack**, since the pack is what goes abroad;
  4. write `empty_at`.

  A refusal changes nothing.
- **The API** (`packages/shared/src/api.ts`, with guards and error codes):
  - `GET /api/holding` gains `writ: WritView` (the stored state plus `writView`'s result at the server's `now`).
  - `POST /api/writ/name` `{ name }` answers the holding, or one of these errors:
    - `name-refused`, with the message the validator gives;
    - `already-named`.
  - `POST /api/writ/feed` `{ itemId, count }` or `{ itemId, fill: true }` answers the holding, or one of these errors:
    - `unnamed`: feeding waits for the Name, as in the script;
    - `not-sparkglass`;
    - `insufficient`;
    - `full`, with the message "Full.".

  The usual rules apply:
  - the Kindled comes from the session cookie only;
  - a mutating route needs a JSON body and a same-origin `Origin`;
  - the guards refuse unknown fields and non-positive or non-integer counts.

  Bump `PROTOCOL_VERSION`: the holding answer's shape changes.
- **The playtest CLI:** `writ:set <user> [--charge-hours N] [--dark-hours N] [--certified on|off]` sets `empty_at` and `certified` for testing a lit, flickering, dark or fading Writ without waiting days. It goes through the holding module (like `holding:away`), and nothing in the game calls it.

  - the charge in words;

### 6. The Writ panel (GAME DESIGN's screen)

**It shows:**
- **The Writ face:** אמת and the Name, drawn with `drawLetters`, each letter in a state:
  - **new:** ember;
  - **lit:** lit;
  - **flickering:** the outer letters (the first and last letter of each line) switch between lit and dim on a fixed beat, while the rest stay lit;
  - **dark and fading:** the aleph dark, so the Writ reads מת, and the Name line dim while dark, then dark once line 1 has faded.

  These are the letter sheet's four states only. ART's M2 UI kit replaces the look later, not the logic.

  A Name wider than the face after the lean hand: the face widens to fit (LORE: "the layout's problem"), never smaller letters.
- **The rim:** "not certified: the Wet House in Malkhut", or "certified".
- **The charge:** the words, with the exact time in a tooltip.
- **The state line:** "Lit", "Flickering", "Dark since <time>, grace ends in <time>" or "Line 1 fading". Times are relative, in hours and minutes, with drawable characters only. The book's "…" is not in the fonts; flag any such character instead of changing text.
- **The lines:** "Line 1: tethers 4 plots" and its state.
- **The next line's needs:** "Line 2: certified, Writ clay, 20 gin", greyed (M2 can't scribe).
- **The Sparkglass in the pack and the store,** by kind.

**The player can feed glass:**
- choose a kind;
- feed one, or Fill;
- before confirming, see a preview of the charge it gives (`feedPreview`: "+⅓ charge, about two days" style; if "⅓" isn't drawable, use "+1/3"). On a full Writ the preview says "Full." and the button is disabled.

**After the answer:** the face relights from the server's answer, never optimistically. On a relight from dark or fading, the aleph returns first, then each faded line returns one after another over a few seconds: whole frames, no alpha.

**Inputs:**
- mouse, pad (Cross confirms, Circle backs out, the D-pad or left stick moves focus) and touch;
- Esc, Circle or the post's Use again closes it.

**Not in 4a** (shown greyed or absent):
- fitting a rim-band;
- scribing a line;
- renaming.
