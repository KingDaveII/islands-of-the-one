# G1.5f: the chest as a sprite: a `prop-chest` table and the engine drawing it

Start after G1.5e (PR #53) is merged. Read CLAUDE.md, `docs/design/sprites.md` (§2, §4, §6), `docs/design/art-pipeline.md` ("Kinds") and `drawChest` in `apps/client/src/render/world.ts`.

Work on the branch `g15f-prop-chest`, which already holds this file (`docs/tasks/g15f.md`). Open one PR, "G1.5f: prop-chest", through the REST API (`gh pr create` is blocked). It is small, and it must merge before ART builds the chest's recipe (art job `tiles-cave`, PR #52).

## Why

A small tiles change rides along (decision 3). The chest is the last thing in an M1 room still drawn by the engine at M1's density. ART is generating it at B, closed and open, and will draw the opening frames by hand. The `prop-` kind only knows `idle` (`PROP_IDLE`), so a chest recipe can't be built yet.

## Decisions (MAIN)

### 1. Prop tables

- A prop may have its own table, like a Husk. Add `PROP_TABLES` next to `HUSK_TABLES` in `packages/shared/src/sprites.ts`, keyed by the name after `prop-`.
- A prop without an entry keeps today's rule: `idle` only, 1 to 8 frames, at the recipe's fps (`PROP_IDLE`).
- `spriteSheetSpec('prop-<name>')` returns the prop's own table when it has one.
- The first entry is `chest`. Every row has no facings and is M1:

| anim | frames | fps | loop | hold |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `closed` | 1 or 2 | the recipe's | yes | no |
| `opening` | 4 | 12 | no | yes |
| `open` | 1 | 0 | no | yes |

- `closed` works like a prop's `idle`, capped at 2 frames: 1 or 2 frames, fps from the recipe (default 8), looping when it has 2. ART draws the clasp glint as its 2 frames (about 2 fps). A 1-frame `closed` is still, with no glint.
- The cell stays the 64×64 prop cell with the origin at (32, 54).
- The pipeline and the §6 sheet test read the table through `spriteSheetSpec`, as they do for Husks:
  - a finished `prop-chest` must carry all three animations;
  - a draft may carry any subset.

### 2. The engine draws the chest from the sheet

- When `prop-chest` is loaded, each chest draws as a sprite at its sim position, through the same render boundary and feet anchor as other props: the chest's soles row sits on its sim position. It is y-sorted with the bodies, as the procedural chest is today.
- The state comes from `ChestState.opened`, which the client only renders:
  - a chest that is closed when the client first sees it shows `closed`;
  - `opened` turning true plays `opening` once on the client clock, then holds `open`;
  - a chest that is already open when the room loads (or when the client first sees it) shows `open`.
- Use the same pattern as the tile doors (`nextDoorView`/`doorFrameAt` in `roomTiles.ts`); share the helper if that's clean.
- Without the sheet, `drawChest` stays as it is. Mark it as the fallback, like the procedural room.
- `closed` loops on the client clock at the sheet's fps; with 1 frame it's still.
- With the sheet, the engine draws no glow of its own over the chest: the blinking clasp goes with the procedural drawing, and any glint is ART's, in `closed`.

### 3. Rarer plain wall variants (ART's answer to G1.5e's questions)

At 50/50 the spare plain face and top read as a pattern, so their pick gets weights like the floor's:
- The sheet's `tiles` block gains `faceMidWeights` and `topMidWeights`: two positive integers each, for `*_mid_0` and `*_mid_1`. Absent means `[1, 1]`.
- `tileAt` picks `face_mid_*` and `top_mid_*` with them, through the same hash and `pickWeighted`. Pass them in the way `floorWeights` is passed today; one weights object for all three is fine.
- Tiles recipes take the same two optional fields and write them into the sheet. ART's `tiles-cave` will use `[3, 1]` for both.
- Tests: the weighted pick, the `[1, 1]` default, and the sheet test's check (two positive integers when present).
- Rebuild the fixture and the review output if they change, and say so.

## Tests

- **`spriteSheetSpec`:** `prop-chest` returns its table, and a prop without one still gets `idle` from the recipe.
- **Pipeline:**
  - a finished `prop-chest` recipe missing `opening` fails;
  - a draft with only `closed` and `open` builds;
  - a prop without a table still builds `idle`.
- **Sheet test:** the chest's frame counts, including a 1- and a 2-frame `closed`, and a 3-frame `closed` failing.
- **Engine, against a fixture `prop-chest`:**
  - closed, opening (every frame) and open from `opened`;
  - a chest that is already open at load shows `open`;
  - the fallback without the sheet.
