## Why

GAME DESIGN, Holding & village (2026-10-19): "Sleep. Lying down by the Writ post is the in-world log-out; the hint says so plainly. It is the SLEEP button at the foot of the Writ panel, with no spot or prompt of its own: the Kindled sits down against the post (the sit row; a lying-down row can come later), the screen cuts to the sleep screen, and the game logs out to the start screen. Sleeping never stops the active action: it runs on offline up to the cap, exactly as when the tab is simply closed, which is never punished either."

GAME DESIGN, Spaces and interaction: "While you were away is not a toast but a card: it opens over the holding on the first log-in after an absence of 30 minutes or more, dismissed by any input." Its M2 lines (the screen list, 2026-10-19): "at most six, in this order: time away; one line per station kind, summed (\"Fields: 56 pale grain, in the baskets\", \"Trees: 18 twisted logs\", \"Outcrop: 40 black stone\", each counting the active action's output with the cycle's); one line for all skill levels gained (\"Woodcutting 7 → 9 · Farming 3 → 4\"); the Writ now." A line with nothing to say is left out. So M2 never passes six lines, and the fold line waits for visitors, the Board and Hearth-warm.

GAME DESIGN, toasts: "A toast is one line, bottom left, above the consumable slots, in the body font with a small glyph for its kind … at most two stack, and it never takes input. Toasts are the only system voice besides hints." The no-fade rule (decision log 2026-10-15): "a toast holds 3 s and then blinks out in one frame; a new toast pushes the older one up one line, two at most." The M2 kinds:

| Kind | When | Text (GAME DESIGN, final) | Glyph (ART, #109) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| skill level | a skill gains a level while you are on the holding | `Woodcutting 2.` (the skill's name and its new level) | `skill-woodcutting`, `skill-mining`, `skill-farming` |
| Writ low | the Writ falls under a day, once per drain | `Your Writ has about a day left.` | `writ-low` |
| Writ dark | the Writ goes dark | `Your Writ reads מת.` | `writ-dark` |
| store full | a take-all leaves some of a stack in the basket | `Store: twisted logs full.` | `store-full` |
| pack full | anything refused as `pack-full` | `Pack full.` | `pack-full` |

"Item gains are not toasts": they stay the "+12" floats over the station.

LORE wrote the lines for sleep and the card (Lore §14.22, decided 2026-10-19). They are quoted below, final. Every line and number goes in data or in one constants module, `packages/shared/src/away.ts` (`AWAY_TEXT`, `SLEEP_TEXT`, `TOAST_TEXT`), which the tests read.

The UI kit isn't in yet. Build the card and the toast strip from the existing panel and text helpers, following GAME DESIGN's layout and ART's glyph placement. ART will restyle them later.

## Decisions (MAIN)

### 1. Data

In `packages/shared/data/holding.json`, validated with the rest:
- `awayCardMinMs`: 1 800 000 (30 min);
- `toastMs`: 3000;
- `toastMax`: 2;
- `sleepSitMs`: 600 (the Kindled sits, or stands if the sheet has no `sit` row, before the cut);
- `sleepMs`: 1500 (how long the sleep screen holds before the log-out lands on the start screen).

A tide is 24 h of server time (Game design "The tide and the lighting"). Put `TIDE_MS` in `away.ts`, with a comment pointing there.

In `words.json`, every Virtue gets LORE's `epithet`. M2 has one Virtue: `yashan` → `"Old"`. The validator requires an epithet on every Virtue and none on a Ground. LORE's whole list is in Lore §14.22, for the words still to come.

### 2. The away summary (server)

The server writes the card; the client only draws it.
- **The absence:** in `settleAll`, before the heartbeat refreshes it, `absence = now − writs.last_seen_at`. That is the holding-wide heartbeat, so every route that settles (the holding routes, and the inventory routes through `settle`) sees the same absence.
- **What is recorded:** when `absence ≥ awayCardMinMs`, the settle records what this stretch made:
  - per station or plot, the item and the count credited to its basket: the active action's `due × quantity` from `settleIn`, plus the cycles from `settleCycles`, summed per place;
  - per skill, the level before the settle and after it (`levelFromXp` on the XP read before and after; only skills whose level changed).
- **Where it is stored:** new table `away_cards`, migration 0009, expand-only. One row per Kindled: `kindled_id` (PK, FK), `from_at` (the last heartbeat before the absence), `to_at` (the settle's `now`), `made` (jsonb: place id → `{ itemId, quantity }`) and `levels` (jsonb: skill → `{ from, to }`). Only `settleAll` writes it; it moves no items, so `inventory.apply` is not involved.
- **Merging:** if a row is still there when another absence is recorded (the card was never dismissed), merge the two: the earlier `from_at`, the later `to_at`, `made` summed per place, and `levels` keeping the earliest `from` and the latest `to`.
- **The answer:** `HoldingResponse` gains `away: AwayCard | null`. `AwayCard` holds `from`, `to`, `made` and `levels`; the Writ line reads the answer's own Writ view, so it says the Writ now. Shared type and guard in `packages/shared/src/api.ts`.
- **Clearing:** a new route `POST /api/holding/away/seen` with body `{ to }` (ISO) deletes the row only if its `to_at` still equals `to`. An absence merged in after the card was drawn survives the dismissal. It answers `204`, guarded like every mutating route (JSON body, same-origin `Origin`, the session cookie).
- Bump `PROTOCOL_VERSION` to 7.

The card shows after any absence of 30 minutes or more, whether the player slept or simply closed the tab. A hidden tab that stops the heartbeat for 30 minutes counts as an absence too. That is GAME DESIGN's rule read literally; say so in holding.md.

### 3. The card's lines (shared, pure)

`awayLines(card, layout, plots, writ, serverMs): string[]` in `away.ts`, in GAME DESIGN's order, each only when it has something to say (six at most in M2):

1. **Time away** (LORE): `Away ` + `duration(ms)` + `.`
   - `duration` gives whole tides first, then hours, with minutes only under an hour, in tens: `1 tide and 3 hours`, `2 tides`, `5 hours`, `40 minutes`.
   - Singular and plural: `1 hour`, `2 hours`, `1 tide`, `2 tides`.
   - Round down. 30 to 39 minutes reads `30 minutes`.
   - A whole number of tides drops the hours (`2 tides`). Tides and minutes never appear together.
2. **One line per station kind, summed** (GAME DESIGN), in the order fields, trees, outcrop:
   - Fields: `Field: 14 pale grain, in the basket` when one field made something, `Fields: 56 pale grain, in the baskets` when several did.
   - Trees: `Trees: 18 twisted logs, in the basket` (the trees share one basket, `twisted-trees`).
   - Outcrop: `Outcrop: 40 black stone, in the basket`.
   - The count includes the active action's output with the cycle's. The item's display name is singular at 1 and plural otherwise (`pluralItemName`).
   - The short kind labels (`Field`/`Fields`, `Trees`, `Outcrop`) go in `AWAY_TEXT`.
   - GAME DESIGN (2026-10-19): "every station line ends with where the things wait: \"basket\" for one station, \"baskets\" for several." The ending counts the baskets the line sums.
3. **Skill levels, one line** (GAME DESIGN): `Woodcutting 7 → 9 · Farming 3 → 4`.
   - Skills in `skills.json` order, joined by ` · `.
   - The arrow is in both atlases now (#109). Test that the line passes `isDrawable`.
4. **The Writ now:**
   - lit or flickering: GAME DESIGN's `Writ: ` + the HUD chip's charge words + ` left` (`Writ: about four days left`, `Writ: less than a day left`);
   - dark in the grace, LORE: `Writ: dark for 5 hours. It still remembers everything.`;
   - faded, LORE: `Writ: dark for 2 tides and 4 hours; its outer line has faded.` With more lines faded (later milestones): `its outer 2 lines have faded`;
   - the dark durations use the same `duration` as the time away;
   - no Writ pressed yet: no line.

LORE's fold line, `More is waiting at the stations.`, goes in `AWAY_TEXT` for later and is not drawn in M2.

Every line passes `isDrawable` and the banned-names test.

**Plot labels (LORE, same decision).** "A station is called by its Virtue's epithet and its kind, so a field named אדמה · ישן is the Old field. A plot with no Virtue is just Field. Only when two still share a label does the plot's number follow it (Field 2), by Writ line order. The same label is used anywhere the Latin UI names a plot."
- Write `plotLabel(plot, plots)` in shared, pure. It is capitalised at the start of a line (`Old field`, `Field`, `Field 2`).
- Use it everywhere the UI names a plot today: the field's station panel title, build mode's header (`Old field, level 2`, through `BUILD_TEXT.HEADER`), and anywhere else a grep for the kind's name finds.
- The away card groups by kind, so it doesn't use the label.

### 4. The card (client)

On a holding answer whose `away` is not null, the card opens over the holding, centred on the UI grid:
- a panel from `ui.ts` with the title `While you were away` and the lines in the body font;
- under them, muted, LORE's last line: `Press anything to wake.`

**Dismissing:**
- The card closes on the first key, mouse button, pad button, stick push past the menu threshold, or touch. That input does nothing else: no step, no interact, no menu.
- While the card is open the Kindled stands still, the world keeps drawing, and the heartbeat keeps running.
- Closing sends `away/seen` with the card's `to`. If that fails, the card stays closed and comes back with the next answer. That is fine.

**Order on landing:** the Name screen first if the island is unnamed (there is no Writ yet, and usually no card), otherwise the card. The toasts wait while the card is open.

### 5. Sleep

**The button:**
- A **SLEEP** button at the foot of the Writ panel (Use on the post). It is the last item in the panel's focus order on the pad, and it is there whether or not the island is named.
- Under it, muted, LORE's hint: `Lie down to sleep and leave the game. Your holding keeps working while you sleep, until the Writ runs low.`

**Pressing SLEEP:**
- The panel closes and the heartbeat stops.
- The Kindled turns to face the post and sits down against it, then holds still for `sleepSitMs`. It plays `sit` (`KINDLED_SHEATHED_ANIMS`: optional, 1 frame held, 2 facings) when the sheet has the row, else holds `idle`. **No sheet has `sit` yet**, so it holds `idle` until ART draws it.
- Then the scene cuts, with no fade, to the hollow background and LORE's line in the body font:
  - `You lie down by the Writ. Its hum is the last thing you hear.`
  - when the Writ is dark: `You lie down by the Writ. It doesn't hum tonight.`
- After `sleepMs` the client sends `POST /api/logout` and lands on the start screen, logged out, the same way StartScene's LOG OUT does.
- Sleep changes nothing on the server: the active action keeps running offline until the cap. If the log-out fails, still land on the start screen; it shows a logged-in visitor as it does today.

Logging in again after 30 minutes or more lands on the holding with the card.

### 6. Toasts (client)

The queue logic is pure and tested in `game/toastView.ts`; `ui/toasts.ts` draws the strip with `render/text.ts`'s `body()`.

**Where the strip sits:**
- bottom left on the UI grid, at `HUD_INSET`, with the newest toast at the bottom;
- add a `toasts` rect to `hudRects`, sized for two lines at the longest M2 text in the body font;
- `render/keshFraming.test.ts` checks that it overlaps neither the chip nor the pack panel at 1280 × 720, 1440 × 810 and 960 × 540;
- the strip counts in `panelsBehind` like the other panels;
- it draws above everything on the UI camera, the inventory and the panels included, and it never takes input (clicks pass through).

**Glyphs (ART, #109):**
- Load `toast-glyphs.json` and its PNG like the item icon sheet (`meta.tinted: false`: draw it untinted).
- ART's placement: "Draw a glyph at 1 UI px per px, vertically centred on its line, one HUD gap before the text." A line is as tall as the 12 px glyph.
- The kind picks the glyph: a skill level the skill's own (`skill-woodcutting` and so on), store full the store's bin, never the item's icon.

**Timing:**
- Each toast holds `toastMs` (3 s), then disappears in one frame. That is GAME DESIGN's "blinks out in one frame": no blink pattern and no alpha.
- A new toast pushes the older one up a line. A third drops the oldest at once.
- A toast with the same text as one already showing restarts that one's 3 s instead of stacking (MAIN).

**The triggers:**
- **Skill level:** a skill's level in a holding answer is higher than in the previous answer of this session. Levels gained while away belong to the card, so the first answer of a session never toasts.
- **Writ low:** the derived charge words become `less than a day` while on the holding, or the Writ is already under a day on landing with no away card showing.
  - Once per drain, remembered per Kindled in `localStorage` (key `iot.toast.writLow.<kindledId>`) as the Writ's `emptyAt` it was shown for. A feed that changes `emptyAt` re-arms it.
  - Wrap every read and write in try/catch. Without storage the toast may repeat, which is fine.
  - When the away card shows a Writ line, mark this drain as told.
- **Writ dark:** the same pattern with its own key, keyed by the `emptyAt` it went dark at.
- **Store full:** the take-all's `take.left` (`takeResultLine`'s text). It replaces the station panel's result line, which goes, along with its blink-and-hold timing.
- **Pack full:** any answer refused as `pack-full`: the inventory's moves and Kesh's tosses. The inventory's refusal line and the talk box's note stop showing "Pack full."; the toast says it instead. Other refusals keep their lines.

Build mode's footer refusals and the Writ panel's refusals stay where they are. GAME DESIGN logged this on 2026-10-19: they aren't toast kinds, and they are panel lines in its screen list. Change holding.md §7's seam, which said the toasts would replace the footer line.
