## Why

GAME DESIGN, Holding & village: "Sleep. Lying down by the Writ post is the in-world log-out; the hint says so plainly. On the next log-in the While you were away card sums up the holding."

GAME DESIGN, Spaces and interaction: "While you were away is not a toast but a card: it opens over the holding on the first log-in after an absence of 30 minutes or more, dismissed by any input." The M2 screen list gives its M2 lines: "time away; what each station made; skill levels gained (\"Woodcutting 7 → 9\"); the Writ now (\"about four days left\" or \"dark since …; the outer line faded\"). Visitors, the Board and Hearth-warm join in later milestones." Each line shows only when it is true, in that order.

GAME DESIGN, toasts: "A toast is one line, bottom left, above the consumable slots, in the body font with a small glyph for its kind … at most two stack, and it never takes input. Toasts are the only system voice besides hints." The no-fade rule (decision log 2026-10-15): "a toast holds 3 s and then blinks out in one frame; a new toast pushes the older one up one line, two at most." The M2 kinds:

## Decisions (MAIN)

### 1. Data

In `packages/shared/data/holding.json`, validated with the rest:
- `awayCardMinMs`: 1 800 000 (30 min);
- `toastMs`: 3000;
- `toastMax`: 2;
- `sleepMs`: 1500 (how long the sleep screen holds before the log-out lands on the start screen).

A tide is 24 h of server time (Game design "The tide and the lighting"). Put `TIDE_MS` in `packages/shared/src/away.ts`, with a comment pointing there.

### 2. The away summary (server)

The server writes the card; the client only draws it.
- **The absence:** in `settleAll`, before the heartbeat refreshes it, `absence = now − writs.last_seen_at`. That is the holding-wide heartbeat, so every route that settles (the holding routes, and the inventory routes through `settle`) sees the same absence.
- **What is recorded:** when `absence ≥ awayCardMinMs`, the settle records what this stretch made:
  - per station or plot, the item and the count credited to its basket: the active action's `due × quantity` from `settleIn`, plus the cycles from `settleCycles`, summed per place;
  - per skill, the level before the settle and after it (`levelFromXp` on the XP read before and after; only skills whose level changed).
- **Where it is stored:** new table `away_cards`, migration 0009, expand-only. One row per Kindled: `kindled_id` (PK, FK), `from_at` (the last heartbeat before the absence), `to_at` (the settle's `now`), `made` (jsonb: place id → `{ itemId, quantity }`) and `levels` (jsonb: skill → `{ from, to }`). Only `settleAll` writes it; it moves no items, so `inventory.apply` is not involved.
- **Merging:** if a row is still there when another absence is recorded (the card was never dismissed), merge the two: the earlier `from_at`, the later `to_at`, `made` summed per place, and `levels` keeping the earliest `from` and the latest `to`.
- **The answer:** `HoldingResponse` gains `away: AwayCard | null`. `AwayCard` holds `from`, `to`, `made` and `levels`, plus the server's `writ` view as it stands, so the Writ line reads the Writ now. Shared type and guard in `packages/shared/src/api.ts`.
- **Clearing:** a new route `POST /api/holding/away/seen` with body `{ to }` (ISO) deletes the row only if its `to_at` still equals `to`. An absence merged in after the card was drawn survives the dismissal. It answers `204`, guarded like every mutating route (JSON body, same-origin `Origin`, the session cookie).
- Bump `PROTOCOL_VERSION` to 7.

The card shows after any absence of 30 minutes or more, whether the player slept or simply closed the tab. A hidden tab that stops the heartbeat for 30 minutes counts as an absence too. That is GAME DESIGN's rule read literally; say so in holding.md.

Use the station's name from `stations.json` ("Twisted trees", "Stone outcrop", "Field") and the item's display name, plural when the count is not 1 (`pluralItemName`). **[LORE]** how to tell two fields apart; placeholder `Field` for the first named plot, `Field 2` and so on for the others, in plot order.
3. **Skill levels gained,** one line per skill: GAME DESIGN's `Woodcutting 7 → 9`. ### 4. The card (client)

On a holding answer whose `away` is not null, the card opens over the holding, centred on the UI grid:
- a panel from `ui.ts` with the title `While you were away` and the lines in the body font;
- under them, muted, **[LORE]** placeholder `Press any key.` That one line is the exception to "no 'press X to'", because the card closes on any input.

**Dismissing:**
- The card closes on the first key, mouse button, pad button, stick push past the menu threshold, or touch. That input does nothing else: no step, no interact, no menu.
- While the card is open the Kindled stands still, the world keeps drawing, and the heartbeat keeps running.
- Closing sends `away/seen` with the card's `to`. If that fails, the card stays closed and comes back with the next answer. That is fine.

**Order on landing:** the Name screen first if the island is unnamed (no Writ line, and usually no away card yet), otherwise the card. The toasts wait while the card is open.

### 5. Sleep

**[GAME DESIGN]** where the player lies down. MAIN's default:
- A **SLEEP** button at the foot of the Writ panel (Use on the post). It is the last item in the panel's focus order on the pad, and it is there whether or not the island is named.
- Under it, muted, the hint that "says so plainly": **[LORE]** placeholder `Lie down here to sleep. Your island keeps working while you're away.`

**Pressing SLEEP:**
- The panel closes and the heartbeat stops. The scene cuts, with no fade, to the hollow background and one line in the body font: **[LORE]** placeholder `You lie down by the Writ.`
- After `sleepMs` the client sends `POST /api/logout` and lands on the start screen, logged out, the same way StartScene's LOG OUT does.
- The active action keeps running offline until the cap; sleep changes nothing on the server. If the log-out fails, still land on the start screen; it shows a logged-in visitor as it does today.
- **[ART]** a `lie_down` row on the sheathed Kindled sheet. Until it exists, the cut is the whole of it.

Logging in again after 30 minutes or more lands on the holding with the card.

### 6. Toasts (client)

`apps/client/src/ui/toasts.ts` holds the logic, pure and tested in `game/toastView.ts`; the strip draws with `render/text.ts`'s `body()`.

**Where the strip sits:**
- bottom left on the UI grid, at `HUD_INSET`, with the newest toast at the bottom;
- add a `toasts` rect to `hudRects`, sized for two lines at the longest M2 text;
- `render/keshFraming.test.ts` checks that it overlaps neither the chip nor the pack panel at 1280 × 720, 1440 × 810 and 960 × 540;
- the strip counts in `panelsBehind` like the other panels;
- it draws above everything on the UI camera, the inventory and the panels included, and it never takes input (clicks pass through).

**Timing:**
- Each toast holds `toastMs` (3 s), then disappears in one frame. That is GAME DESIGN's "blinks out in one frame": no blink pattern and no alpha.
- A new toast pushes the older one up a line. A third drops the oldest at once.
- A toast with the same text as one already showing restarts that one's 3 s instead of stacking (MAIN).

**Glyphs:** **[ART]** a glyph for each of the five kinds, from the M2 UI kit. Placeholders:
- store full: the item's own icon from the item icon sheet;
- skill level: the icon of the item the skill makes (Woodcutting a twisted log, Mining black stone, Farming pale grain);
- pack full, Writ low and Writ dark: an 8 × 8 square of the `uiInkMuted` token.

Name them in the PR for ART.

**The triggers:**
- **Skill level:** a skill's level in a holding answer is higher than in the previous answer of this session. Levels gained while away belong to the card, so the first answer of a session never toasts.
- **Writ low:** the derived charge words become `less than a day` while on the holding, or the Writ is already under a day on landing with no away card showing. Once per drain, remembered per Kindled in `localStorage` (key `iot.toast.writLow.<kindledId>`) as the Writ's `emptyAt` it was shown for. A feed that changes `emptyAt` re-arms it. Wrap every read and write in try/catch: without storage the toast may repeat, which is fine. When the away card shows a Writ line, mark this drain as told.
- **Writ dark:** the same pattern with its own key, keyed by the `emptyAt` it went dark at.
- **Store full:** the take-all's `take.left` (`takeResultLine`'s text). It replaces the station panel's result line, which goes, along with its blink-and-hold timing.
- **Pack full:** any answer refused as `pack-full`: the inventory's moves and Kesh's tosses. The inventory's refusal line and the talk box's note stop showing "Pack full."; the toast says it instead. Other refusals keep their lines.

Build mode's footer refusals and the Writ panel's refusals stay where they are. GAME DESIGN's list of M2 toast kinds doesn't include them, and they are panel lines in its screen list. Change holding.md §7's seam, which said the toasts would replace the footer line, and tell GAME DESIGN in the PR.
