## Why

M2 starts the player on the holding with no voyages, so GAME DESIGN's only source of Sparkglass in M2 is Kesh:
- **his first sliver** (LORE §14.3, "First one's free. Don't get used to it.");
- **one more each tide**, behind the `m2Scaffold` data flag, which goes away in M3 when caves and wrecks give glass.

He is also the holding's first voice: LORE's lines after the island is named, after the first feeding, and four topics to ask him about.

ART's art is on main:
- `npc-kesh`, seated on his rock: `fish`, `talk` and `toss`, with `hand` on every toss frame (#93);
- `prop-kesh-rock`, with a perch cut into its tip;
- `prop-sparkglass`, the pickup chip.

Everything player-facing below is LORE's or GAME DESIGN's, word for word. Don't reword it. Stage directions in parentheses are part of the line as LORE wrote it; draw them as text.

## Decisions (MAIN)

### 1. Kesh in the world

- **Load and draw `npc-kesh`:** add it to `SPRITE_SHEET_NAMES` and `spriteSheets`. Draw it at `prop-kesh-rock`'s world point (the bottom centre of `layout.kesh.rock`), one depth step above the rock, with no shadow. There is no tone swap: his clay is drawn in.
- **His rows:**
  - `fish` loops by default;
  - `talk` loops while a talk box with his line is open;
  - `toss` plays once at its rate, then back to `fish`.

  All are facing-less (`fish`, `talk`, `toss` as keys).
- **The toss:**
  - When `toss` reaches `toss_2` (`KESH_TOSS_RELEASE_FRAME`), the thrown thing leaves his hand.
  - It is drawn from its own sprite, `prop-sparkglass`'s `idle` for a sliver. It flies in a whole-pixel arc from that frame's `hand` point (`framePoint`, mapped like the Hookblade's points: the sprite's anchor + (point − cell origin)) to the Kindled's feet.
  - Use about 600 ms, with the height of the arc in tuning (`kesh.tossMs`, `kesh.tossArcPx`), not in code.
  - Where it lands, the cycles' float shows "+1".
  - The flight is cosmetic: the count comes from the server's answer (§3), never from the animation.
- **Camera margin:** the PACK chip covered the rock in a 1440 × 810 window (ART), because the camera clamps to the template's edge and the rock sits two tiles from it.
  - Add `cameraMarginTiles` (6) to `holding.json`. `followCamera` passes it to `clampScroll` as `near` and `far` on both axes, so the camera may scroll that far into the void past every edge.
  - Test, as pure layout math: with the Kindled standing on `kesh.talk`, the drawn rect of the rock and of Kesh (their frames' opaque bounds at the world fit) doesn't intersect the HUD's rects (the top-left Writ block, the top-right purse, the bottom-centre chip and the bottom-right PACK chip) at 1280 × 720, 1440 × 810 and 960 × 540.

### 2. Talking (client)

- **The prompt:** standing on `kesh.talk`, or within the same reach the stations use, shows the interact prompt with the verb **TALK** (beside 3b's USE, as data like `USE_LABEL`). Use opens the **talk box**.
- **The talk box:** a placeholder in the existing panel style, bottom of the screen; ART's UI kit replaces the look later.
  - It shows one line at a time: the speaker ("Kesh", or nothing for the Narrator) and the line. Narrator lines are in the muted style (`readability.md`); a hint line has no speaker and the hint style.
  - Confirm (Cross, click, tap, the interact key) advances; Circle or Esc closes it at any point.
  - While a Kesh line is shown, `npc-kesh` plays `talk`.
  - The world keeps running behind it, but the Kindled doesn't move while it is open.
- **The menu:** at the top level the box offers the topics, in this order, then ends:
  1. **The Old Letters**
  2. **Sparkglass**
  3. **Where the crack points**
  4. **Goodbye**

  When a sliver is due (§3), Talk first plays the due toss, then the menu.
- **The lines:** they live in `packages/shared/data/kesh.json`, validated on load, word for word. Every line passes `DRAWABLE_CHARS`; flag a character the fonts lack instead of changing the text. A line is `{ "who": "kesh" | "narrator" | "hint", "text": "…" }`, optionally with an action (`"toss": "sliver"`). Flows:
  - `afterNaming` (plays from `afterNaming()` once the server accepts the Name, after 4a's flare):
    - Kesh: "(squints at the post) "Hm. That's what you're calling it?" (shrugs) "Doesn't matter. Whatever you call it, it's yours now. The Hollow doesn't care if it's pretty.""
    - Narrator: "I've heard worse, little light. Someone once named theirs Rock."
    - Hint: "Your Writ keeps your island's name for two days even while you're away. After that it doesn't break. It only forgets, a line at a time, until you bring Sparkglass to make it remember."
  - `afterFirstFeeding` (plays from `afterFeeding()` the first time a feed lands, once per Kindled; `writ.fedAt` below tells the client):
    - Kesh: ""There. Now it'll hold while you're gone. Two days, give or take. After that it starts forgetting the edges first.""
  - Topic **The Old Letters** (Kesh, one line per step):
    - ""Old Letters. The writing from before. Everything out here that lasts is written in it: Writs, sails, the stones on the roads. Things written in anything else go soft, like your stick out there.""
    - ""Reads right to left. Starts where your hand doesn't expect. You'll get used to it.""
    - ""Scriptorium lot say the world was said before it was made. That the light spelled everything as it fell." (snorts) "I say it's a good way to sell tiles.""
    - ""You learn a word, you've got it for good. Tiles, rubbings off the waymark stones, scraps off old tablets. Every word's a tool.""
    - ""Out on the roads there are stones with one letter each. Put paper to one and rub. That's your first letter for your sail.""
    - ""Want to know more, ask the Scriptorium in Malkhut. Ask for the archivist. Don't let her charge you.""
    - Narrator: "He's right about the tiles. He's wrong about the rest. Or he's right, and it's worse."
  - Topic **Sparkglass**:
    - Kesh: "(holds up a sliver; it glows faint amber-white, the glow moving inside like a slow pulse)" as its own line;
    - Kesh: ""Light. What's left of it. When everything broke, most of the light went up. Some didn't. Out there it goes cold and hard and you get this.""
    - Kesh: ""You find it in wrecks. On the cold edges. Inside the eels and worse, when you open them.""
    - Kesh: ""Feed it to your Writ and the letters stay lit. Don't, and they go dark. You've seen what dark is.""
    - Kesh: ""It's money too. Cities cut it into beads and roll their seal round them. Sealed glass. Raw stuff like this, they'll take, but they weigh it short.""
    - Kesh: ""So: burn it or spend it. Can't do both with the same bit." / "Richest man I ever met had a Writ black as a boot. Old joke.""
    - **Only while the first sliver hasn't been given:** Kesh: "(flicks the sliver across the water) "First one's free. Don't get used to it."", with `"toss": "sliver"`. Then the hint "Touch Sparkglass to your Writ to feed it."
    - Narrator: "Burn it, spend it. Nobody ever asks where it goes when it burns, little light. Nobody."
  - Topic **Where the crack points** (Kesh, then the Narrator):
    - ""Malkhut. The Kingdom. Nearest of the big cities, and the lowest. Every new one ends up there sooner or later.""
    - ""Ten of them, the big cities, each sat on something huge that holds the rock together. Pillars, the clerks call them. Malkhut's got a wheel. A great brass wheel full of eyes. It blinks.""
    - ""Ziggurats. Clerks. A hearth the size of a house, with that painting over it. More seals than sense.""
    - ""Every crack on every rock I've seen points at the nearest city. Don't ask me why. I asked once. Got a sermon.""
    - ""Follow the line. You'll see the lamps before long. Just outside the city there's a stone standing in the light on its own, one mark cut in it. Take your rubbing off it. That's the start of the only road out of the Kingdom.""
    - ""They'll want you registered. They'll want your glass. Give 'em one of the two.""
    - Narrator: "The Kingdom, little light. Lowest of the ten, and the only one with a single road out. Everybody starts there. Nobody is told why."
  - Topic **Goodbye**:
    - Kesh: ""Goodbye's a big word for a stone's throw. I'll be here. I'm always here.""
    - Kesh: ""Mind the eels. Mind the wrecks more. Wrecks have people in them, or had.""
    - Kesh: "(for the first time, looking straight at the player) "And if something gets you out there, you'll wake up back on your own rock, lighter in the pockets. Don't ask me how. Hurts, though. Every time." / "What falls out of your pockets doesn't vanish. The haze keeps it. Remember that.""
    - Kesh: ""Bring me something odd from the light and I'll trade you a word for it.""
    - Narrator: "Wave to him, little light. He'll pretend not to see. He sees everything."
  - **The tide's sliver** (`m2Scaffold`, §3): Kesh: "(flicks a sliver across the water) "Don't get used to it."", with `"toss": "sliver"`.

  Kesh's lines that belong to later tasks stay out: the word tile (אדמה) and the seeds are Task 5's, the codex entries come with the codex, and odd catches come with voyages. Write the quotation marks in the data exactly as above. The outer pair is the data's string and is not drawn; the inner quotes are LORE's speech marks and are drawn.

### 3. Slivers from Kesh (server)

- **A new data file, `packages/shared/data/scaffold.json`:** `{ "m2Scaffold": true }`, validated on load, read by the server and the client.
- **The tide, in data (`packages/shared/data/tide.json`):** `fullOfTide` "20:00" and `timeZone` "Europe/Berlin" (GAME DESIGN: "The full of the tide is a fixed hour chosen for the server's players").
  - Write `tideIndex(date)` in shared: tide n begins at the full of the tide.
  - It is computed with `Intl.DateTimeFormat` in that time zone, so the full is 20:00 local on both sides of a daylight-saving change, and a tide is 23 or 25 h on those two days.
  - Also `nextFullOfTide(date)`, for the client and the tests.
- **Storage:** a new table `kesh_gifts`, keyed by the Kindled (cascade): `first_sliver_at` (null) and `last_tide` (integer, null, the tide index of the last tide's sliver). The migration is expand-only, and only the holding module touches it.
- **`POST /api/kesh/sliver`, `{}`:**
  1. Locks the Kindled (as the holding's requests do).
  2. If the first sliver hasn't been given, gives it and sets `first_sliver_at`.
  3. Otherwise, if `m2Scaffold` is on and `last_tide` is not the current tide, gives one and sets `last_tide`.
  4. Otherwise refuses with `not-due`.

  Each answers the holding.
  - **The sliver:** one `wild-sliver` into the **pack** through `inventory.apply`, with a new reason `gift` (add it to `InventoryReason` and the ledger docs).
  - **A full pack:** refuse with `pack-full` ("Pack full."), and the sliver stays due.
  - **The first sliver doesn't use up the tide's:** a player who gets the first one today can get the tide's sliver too. GAME DESIGN wants a Writ to go dark two days after the first sliver unless fed, and the tide's sliver is the M2 way to keep it fed.
  - The Kindled comes from the session cookie. A mutating route needs a JSON body and a same-origin `Origin`. The guard refuses any field.
- **`GET /api/holding` gains `kesh`:**
  - `firstGiven` (boolean);
  - `due` (`first`, `tide` or null);
  - `nextTideAt` (ISO, the next full of the tide).

  The client decides which flow to play from this.
- **The first feeding, `writ.fedAt`:** if 4a doesn't already expose when a Writ was first fed, add a nullable `fed_at` to `writs`, expand-only, set by the first feed. The client plays `afterFirstFeeding` only when it went from null to set in the feed's answer.
- **The grant CLI** keeps working for playtests. Add `kesh:reset <user>` (clears both columns, through the holding module) so a playtest can see the first sliver again.
- Bump `PROTOCOL_VERSION`.
