## Why

GAME DESIGN, Encounters & dungeons: "The first weapon comes from the rack in the first wreck (Lore §14.5, §16.5): all three hang there, the player takes one, and the other two are carried home to the weapon rack at the holding, where every weapon owned hangs. One weapon goes on a run, chosen at the rack before leaving; there is no swapping mid-run."

LORE, §14.15 (home again): "The weapon rack. A rack of twisted logs lashed up by the post; the two weapons not taken from the wreck hang there." Its tooltip: `Weapon rack: Every weapon you own hangs here. Take one out with you; the others wait.`

LORE, §16.5, the cards:

| Weapon | Card |
| --- | --- |
| Hookblade | A short curved blade with a hook on its back and a coil of cord at the pommel. For getting close, and pulling things closer. |
| Sling | A leather sling and a pouch of clay pellets, each pellet pressed with a mark. For keeping your distance. |
| Keelmaul | A maul made from a piece of ship's keel, banded in iron. Heavy. For when there's no going around. |

M2 has no wreck (voyages come in M3), and only the Hookblade fights in the sim (the Sling and the Keelmaul are "after the Hookblade set; MAIN schedules"). So the rack in M2 is a stand-in for the wreck's gift. It holds what you own, and choosing at it decides the weapon a run uses.

## Decisions (MAIN)

### 1. Weapons are items

A weapon is something of value the server decides. So it is an item in the ledger, changed only through `inventory.apply`.
- `ITEM_KINDS` gains `weapon`. `items.json` gains `hookblade`, `sling` and `keelmaul`:
  - LORE's names and cards, word for word;
  - plurals `hookblades`, `slings`, `keelmauls`;
  - stack limit 1;
  - no skill.
- An item id of kind `weapon` must be a key of `tuning.json`'s `weapons` or an entry of its `weaponRoster`. Test it.
- Rename the roster's placeholders `locked-2` and `locked-3` to `sling` and `keelmaul`, still `locked: true`, so the roster and the items share ids. Check every use of the old ids. Leave `packages/sim/test/fixtures/golden-tuning.json` as it is: the replay goldens must not move for a UI rename. If they would, say so in the PR instead.
- A new fixed place, `rack`, holds weapons and nothing else, one of each. `inventory.apply` refuses:
  - a weapon anywhere but the rack (`wrong-place`);
  - anything else on the rack (`wrong-place`);
  - a second of one weapon (`stack-limit`).
- Weapons take no pack slot and never show in the inventory's panes. The rack is where they are seen.
- A new reason, `kit`: what a Kindled is given to start with.

**[GAME DESIGN]** what hangs on the rack in M2. MAIN's default:
- Only the Hookblade, given once onto the rack (reason `kit`, idempotent ref `kit:<kindledId>:hookblade`) by the first holding request of a Kindled who owns no weapon.
- This is behind `m2Scaffold`, like Kesh's tide sliver: M3's wreck replaces it.
- The Sling and the Keelmaul aren't owned in M2, because they can't fight yet.
- If GAME DESIGN answers "all three", grant all three the same way. The two that can't fight then show as owned but not ready (§3).

### 2. The choice

- `kindled.run_weapon`: a nullable text column, migration 0010, expand-only. Null means "the first weapon in roster order that the Kindled owns and that can fight" (`isWeaponAvailable`).
- `POST /api/holding/rack/choose { weaponId }` sets it. It answers the holding, or refuses with:
  - `not-owned` (not on the Kindled's rack);
  - `not-ready` (owned, but the sim can't run it);
  - `bad-request`.
  - Guarded like every mutating route.
- `HoldingResponse` gains `rack: { weapons: { itemId, ready }[]; chosen: string | null }`: the Kindled's weapons in roster order, and the weapon a run takes, resolved (never null while they own one that can fight). Shared type and guard in `api.ts`.
- Bump `PROTOCOL_VERSION` to 8.

### 3. The rack on the holding

**The spot:** drop `reserved: true` from the `weapon-rack` spot (2 × 1 at col 17, row 13, beside the post). It becomes an interactable station of kind `rack`, actionless like the post and the store, with LORE's tooltip.

**The prop:**
- **[ART]** a `prop-weapon-rack` sheet.
- MAIN's contract for it: optional (`OPTIONAL_SPRITE_SHEETS`), a cell of its own in `PROP_CELLS` (64 × 72, origin (32, 64), on the bottom centre of its 2 × 1 rect like every prop), `idle` alone, the prop rule's shadow.
- Add it to `STATION_SHEETS` as optional.
- Until the sheet lands, the rack draws a placeholder box of its rect in the piece tokens (`pieceFace`, `pieceShade`, a 1 px outline), the same fallback as the pieces.
- The weapons hanging on it are not drawn in M2. The panel shows them.

**The panel** (Use on the rack; `ui/rackPanel.ts`, logic pure in `game/rackView.ts`). **[GAME DESIGN]** the rack screen: it isn't in the M2 screen list. MAIN's default:
- Title `Weapon rack`, then LORE's tooltip line, muted.
- One row per weapon in roster order. Each row has:
  - the weapon's name;
  - its card, muted, wrapped;
  - on the right, its state:
    - the chosen weapon: `Chosen` (highlighted);
    - an owned weapon that can fight: a **CHOOSE** button;
    - an owned weapon that can't fight yet: greyed, **[LORE]** default `Not ready yet.`;
    - a weapon not owned: no row (M2's default owns only the Hookblade, so the panel shows one row).
- A refusal is one line in the panel's footer with the inventory's blink-and-hold timing (panel refusals stay panel lines).
- Pad: Up/Down move the focus through the rows, Cross chooses, Circle closes. Mouse and touch as the Writ panel.

**[LORE]** the words:
- the button: default `CHOOSE`;
- the chosen mark: default `Chosen`;
- the not-ready line: default `Not ready yet.`;
- the start screen's line under the weapon (§4): default `Chosen at your weapon rack.`

### 4. The start screen

The rack decides now: the start screen's CHOOSE YOUR WEAPON picker goes.
- START RUN reads `rack.chosen` from a holding answer (fetch it when the screen opens), and the run starts with that weapon.
- Where the picker was, the weapon's name shows with **[LORE]**'s line under it.
- If the answer can't be had (the server is down), START RUN uses the roster's first weapon that can fight, as today, and the line says nothing.

**[GAME DESIGN]** where a run starts in M2. MAIN's default: START RUN stays on the start screen until boats (M3) give the holding its landing. Nothing on the holding starts a run.
