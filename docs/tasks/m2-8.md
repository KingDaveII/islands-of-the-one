## Why

GAME DESIGN, Encounters & dungeons: "The first weapon comes from the rack in the first wreck (Lore §14.5, §16.5): all three hang there, the player takes one, and the other two are carried home to the weapon rack at the holding, where every weapon owned hangs. One weapon goes on a run, chosen at the rack before leaving; there is no swapping mid-run."

LORE, §14.15 (home again): "The weapon rack. A rack of twisted logs lashed up by the post; the two weapons not taken from the wreck hang there." Its tooltip: `Weapon rack: Every weapon you own hangs here. Take one out with you; the others wait.`

LORE, §16.5, the cards:

| Weapon | Card |
| --- | --- |
| Hookblade | A short curved blade with a hook on its back and a coil of cord at the pommel. For getting close, and pulling things closer. |
| Sling | A leather sling and a pouch of clay pellets, each pellet pressed with a mark. For keeping your distance. |
| Keelmaul | A maul made from a piece of ship's keel, banded in iron. Heavy. For when there's no going around. |

M2 has no wreck (voyages come in M3), and only the Hookblade fights in the sim (the Sling and the Keelmaul are "after the Hookblade set; MAIN schedules"). So the rack in M2 is a stand-in for the wreck's gift. It holds what you own, and choosing at it decides the weapon a run uses.

GAME DESIGN, LORE and ART answered MAIN's questions on 2026-10-19 (Game design's M2 screen list, row "Weapon rack"; Lore §14.15, "Weapon rack lines"; ART's contract), and this file includes their answers. GAME DESIGN:
- "In M2 the rack holds the Hookblade alone, given once on arrival behind m2Scaffold, and chosen; in M3 the wreck takes over, and a Kindled who already has the Hookblade finds the other two there."
- "One weapon is always chosen."
- "Runs still start from START RUN on the start screen until boats give the holding its landing; that run takes the weapon chosen here, with no picker of its own."

Every player-facing line goes in one constants module, `RACK_TEXT` in `packages/shared/src/rack.ts`, or in data, word for word.

## Decisions (MAIN)

### 1. Weapons are items

A weapon is something of value the server decides. So it is an item in the ledger, changed only through `inventory.apply`.
- `ITEM_KINDS` gains `weapon`. `items.json` gains `hookblade`, `sling` and `keelmaul`:
  - LORE's names and cards, word for word;
  - plurals `hookblades`, `slings`, `keelmauls`;
  - stack limit 1;
  - no skill;
  - `kit`: the weapon's moves in input order, as the rack shows them (GAME DESIGN). Hookblade: `["Arc", "Hook-thrust", "Haul"]`. The Sling and the Keelmaul get theirs when their combat lands.
    - The validator requires a non-empty `kit` on a weapon that can fight and allows an empty one on a locked weapon.
    - Only kind `weapon` may carry `kit`.
- An item id of kind `weapon` must be a key of `tuning.json`'s `weapons` or an entry of its `weaponRoster`. Test it.
- Rename the roster's placeholders `locked-2` and `locked-3` to `sling` and `keelmaul`, still `locked: true`, so the roster and the items share ids. Check every use of the old ids. Leave `packages/sim/test/fixtures/golden-tuning.json` as it is: the replay goldens must not move for a UI rename. If they would, say so in the PR instead.
- A new fixed place, `rack`, holds weapons and nothing else, one of each. `inventory.apply` refuses:
  - a weapon anywhere but the rack (`wrong-place`);
  - anything else on the rack (`wrong-place`);
  - a second of one weapon (`stack-limit`).
- Weapons take no pack slot and never show in the inventory's panes. The rack is where they are seen.
- A new reason, `kit`: what a Kindled is given to start with.

What hangs on the rack in M2 (GAME DESIGN):
- Only the Hookblade, given once onto the rack (reason `kit`, idempotent ref `kit:<kindledId>:hookblade`) by the first holding request of a Kindled who owns no weapon.
- This is behind `m2Scaffold`, like Kesh's tide sliver: M3's wreck replaces it.
- The Sling and the Keelmaul aren't owned in M2. The not-ready state (§3) is built and tested, but no M2 player sees it.

### 2. The choice

- `kindled.run_weapon`: a nullable text column, migration 0010, expand-only. Null means "the first weapon in roster order that the Kindled owns and that can fight" (`isWeaponAvailable`).
- `POST /api/holding/rack/choose { weaponId }` sets it. It answers the holding, or refuses with:
  - `not-owned` (not on the Kindled's rack);
  - `not-ready` (owned, but the sim can't run it);
  - `bad-request`.
  - Guarded like every mutating route.
- `HoldingResponse` gains `rack: { weapons: { itemId, ready }[]; chosen: string | null }`: the Kindled's weapons in roster order, and the weapon a run takes, resolved. "One weapon is always chosen" (GAME DESIGN): `chosen` is null only while the Kindled owns no weapon that can fight, which M2's kit never allows. Shared type and guard in `api.ts`.
- Bump `PROTOCOL_VERSION` to 8.

### 3. The rack on the holding

**The spot:** drop `reserved: true` from the `weapon-rack` spot (2 × 1 at col 17, row 13, beside the post). It becomes an interactable station of kind `rack`, actionless like the post and the store, with LORE's tooltip.

**The prop.** ART confirmed MAIN's contract for `prop-weapon-rack` and is drawing it now ("lashed twisted logs in the twisted tree's bronze and brass-dark, bound with sleeper-ochre rope", at most 62 px wide and about 40 px tall). Write the contract:
- optional (`OPTIONAL_SPRITE_SHEETS`), a cell of its own in `PROP_CELLS`: 64 × 72, origin (32, 64), on the bottom centre of its 2 × 1 rect like every prop;
- `idle` alone, with the prop rule's shadow;
- in `STATION_SHEETS` for the `rack` kind, drawn when on disk;
- three optional named points, `peg_0`, `peg_1`, `peg_2` (ART: "Please reserve the pegs in the contract now"). This sheet may carry `points` the way `npc-kesh` carries `hand`; the sheet test allows them on this prop alone, each a point inside the cell.
- In M2 nothing hangs on the pegs: the equipped weapon is under the cloak and the Hookblade is the only weapon (ART). Later each unequipped weapon gets a small "hung" sprite drawn at a peg, one rack frame however many weapons come. Write that into sprites.md as the plan, but don't build it.
- Until the sheet lands, the rack draws a placeholder box of its rect in the piece tokens (`pieceFace`, `pieceShade`, a 1 px outline), the same fallback as the pieces. If ART's sheet is on `main` before you finish, check it draws and retake shot 1 with it.

**The panel** (Use on the rack; `ui/rackPanel.ts`, logic pure in `game/rackView.ts`), GAME DESIGN's row with LORE's words:
- Title `Weapon rack`, then LORE's tooltip line, muted: `Every weapon you own hangs here. Take one out with you; the others wait.`
- One row per weapon owned, in roster order. Each row has:
  - the weapon's name;
  - its card, muted, wrapped;
  - its kit in input order, joined by ` · ` (`Arc · Hook-thrust · Haul`);
  - on the right, its state:
    - the weapon going out next run: LORE's `Taken` (highlighted);
    - an owned weapon that can fight: LORE's **TAKE** button;
    - an owned weapon that can't fight yet: greyed, LORE's `Your hands aren't ready for this one yet.` (see the note below);
    - a weapon not owned: no row. In M2 the panel shows one row, the Hookblade, Taken.
- A refusal is one line in the panel's footer with the inventory's blink-and-hold timing (panel refusals stay panel lines).
- Pad: Up/Down move the focus through the rows, Cross chooses, Circle closes. Mouse and touch as the Writ panel.

**The not-ready line** has two answers, and neither is ever shown in M2:
- GAME DESIGN's row reuses the book's Found-weapons line, `It needs a smith who knows it`. In the book that line is for a *rusted* weapon of a kind the game can't play yet.
- LORE wrote `Your hands aren't ready for this one yet.` for this state.

Use LORE's, since player-facing words are LORE's, and keep GAME DESIGN's in `RACK_TEXT` as `NEEDS_SMITH` for the rusted state that comes with the Forge. Ask both in the PR to confirm.

GAME DESIGN's row still says CHOOSE and Chosen. LORE's TAKE and Taken are the words; note it in the PR so GAME DESIGN updates its row.

**Kesh** (LORE, in M2 already): a new topic in `kesh.json`, `rack`, titled `The rack`, before Goodbye, always open. Its three lines are Kesh's, word for word:
1. `"Pick one before you go. One blade, one trip. Nobody fights with a rack on their back."`
2. `"Hook for close. Sling for far. The maul for anything that won't get out of the way."`
3. `"Get yourself killed out there and you'll wake by your post, and it'll be hanging right here. What's yours finds its way home. Glass doesn't, not all of it."`

The title wording is MAIN's; ask LORE to confirm it in the PR.

### 4. The start screen

The rack decides now: the start screen's CHOOSE YOUR WEAPON picker goes.
- START RUN reads `rack.chosen` from a holding answer (fetch it when the screen opens), and the run starts with that weapon.
- Where the picker was, the weapon's name shows with LORE's line under it: `Taken from your weapon rack.`
- If the answer can't be had (the server is down), START RUN uses the roster's first weapon that can fight, as today, and the line says nothing.

Where a run starts (GAME DESIGN): START RUN stays on the start screen until boats (M3) give the holding its landing. Nothing on the holding starts a run.
