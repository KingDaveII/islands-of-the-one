Work on the branch `m2-3c-shore-tiles`, which already holds this file (`docs/tasks/m2-3c.md`). Open one PR, "M2 Task 3c: shore tiles, doorless sets, tall props", through the REST API (`gh pr create` is blocked).

## Why

#75 drew the holding with the cave's tile rule. The cave rule puts a wall face where a solid cell has open ground to its south, which suits walls rising up. For an island seen at a low ¾ angle it draws backwards: the north edge shows a cliff rising, and the south edge shows the Hollow's dark tops.

ART's answers (2026-10-18):
- island tile sets get a **shore rule**;
- tile sets may have **no doors**;
- **tall props**, such as trees, get a taller cell.

This task makes the contract and the engine side, and updates MAIN's placeholders so ART can draw the real pieces in the holding art job.

## Decisions

### 1. A tile set declares its rule

The sheet's `tiles` block, and the recipe that writes it, gain `"rule": "cave" | "shore"`, defaulting to `"cave"`. `tiles-cave` stays `"cave"` and doesn't change; `tiles-holding` becomes `"shore"`. `tileAt` dispatches on the rule. Keep the cave rule's code and tests exactly as they are.

### 2. The shore rule (ART's design)

- **Ground and void:**
  - **Ground** is every cell that isn't the island's edge or outside the template. Station cells (`W`, `T`, `R`) are ground for drawing, though solid for collision. The sim's collision doesn't change.
  - **Void** is the edge (`#`) and everything outside.
- **Floor:** a ground cell draws `floor_0` to `floor_3`, by the same hash and `floorWeights`. No shades on a shore set.
- **Lips:** overlays on a ground cell beside the void, which stack. Each is the outline and a lit rim, with no face:
  - `lip_n`: void to its north, the island's back edge, which simply ends;
  - `lip_w`, `lip_e`: void to its west or east;
  - `lip_nw`: NW is void while N and W are ground, an inner corner. `lip_ne` likewise.
  - No south lip: the drop below covers it.
- **Drops:** a void cell with ground directly north draws a drop, the cliff hanging below the island into the Hollow:
  - `drop_mid_0` or `drop_mid_1`: both W and E neighbours are drop cells too. Picked by the hash with `dropMidWeights`, which defaults to `[1, 1]`; `tiles-holding` sets `[3, 1]`.
  - `drop_w`: the cliff ends on its west side, because the west neighbour isn't a drop cell. `drop_e` likewise, and `drop_we` when both ends do.
- **Outer corners:** a void cell with no ground north but ground at NE draws `drop_corner_w`, the cliff turning round the corner west of the run. With ground at NW instead, it draws `drop_corner_e`.
  - A void cell with ground at both NE and NW but not N is a one-cell notch the pieces can't draw. **The layout validator refuses it**, naming the cell, so a layout never needs it.
- **Every other void cell draws nothing:** the Hollow shows through. The room texture is transparent there over the hollow background, as the margin is now.

Put the shore piece names in `packages/shared/src/tiles.ts` beside the cave's, and the required set per rule:
- **cave:** today's set;
- **shore:**
  - the floors;
  - `drop_mid_0`, `drop_mid_1`, `drop_w`, `drop_e`, `drop_we`, `drop_corner_w`, `drop_corner_e`;
  - `lip_n`, `lip_w`, `lip_e`, `lip_nw`, `lip_ne`.

**Opacity:** the floors and the drops are opaque at the top, but a drop's dither into hollow-deep may end in transparent rows. Lips are transparent overlays. State the opacity checks per piece as the cave's are.

### 3. Doors are optional per tile set (ART)

- A new `"doors": true | false` in the recipe and the sheet's `tiles` block. It defaults to `true`, and `tiles-cave` keeps its doors.
- A set with `"doors": false` carries no door pieces. The sheet test and the pipeline require none and refuse any present.
- **Test:** a template drawn with a doorless set has no `D` in it. A template that needs doors with such a set fails, naming the template.
- `tiles-holding` becomes `"doors": false` and drops its placeholder doors.

### 4. Tall props (ART)

- **Some props get a tall cell:** 64 × 96 with the origin at (32, 86), the line under the trunk. The canopy overhangs upwards, and the ground-line sort from G1.5h puts the Kindled behind it.
- **How it's declared:** in shared code, as a per-prop cell beside `PROP_TABLES` (for example `PROP_CELLS`), not in the recipe. The first is `prop-twisted-tree`.
- `spriteSheetSpec` returns the tall cell for those names. The pipeline places frames on it, and the sheet test checks it.
- **Unchanged:** the Writ post and the stone outcrop keep the 64 × 64 prop cell, and the shadow rule stays as it is.
- **Collision** stays the layout's 2 × 2 footprint.

### 5. MAIN's placeholders, updated

`tools/placeholder-holding.py` makes the shore set's placeholder pieces:
- `tiles-holding`:
  - `"rule": "shore"`, `"doors": false`, `dropMidWeights [3, 1]`;
  - initials on the drops and lips only, not on floors;
  - the drop a lit lip, a short rock face, then a dither into hollow-deep, roughly as ART describes, so shots read right.
- `prop-twisted-tree` on the tall cell.

ART replaces all of it in the holding art job; the recipes say so.

## Don't

- Don't change the cave rule, `tiles-cave` or any room in `rooms.json`.
- Don't touch the sim, the holding's server code or the inventory.
- Don't draw real art.
